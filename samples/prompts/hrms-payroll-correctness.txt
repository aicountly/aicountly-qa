HRMS payroll and attendance correctness run.

1. Create employees with known joining dates, salary structures and leave balances.
2. Mark attendance and apply leave for a known period, including a partial day and a holiday overlap.
3. Run the payroll cycle for that period.
4. Open payslips, the payroll register and statutory reports.

Verify:
- Gross pay equals the sum of the configured earning components.
- Deductions (PF, ESI, TDS, professional tax) are computed on the correct base and match the configured rate.
- Net pay equals gross minus total deductions on every payslip.
- The payroll register total equals the sum of the individual payslips.
- Leave balance after approval equals opening balance minus days taken.
- Attendance days used by payroll equal the days marked.

Report every mismatch with employee reference, expected value and printed value, plus all console errors, failed API calls and forms that fail to save.
