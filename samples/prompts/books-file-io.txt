Bank statement import/export correctness on Smart Books.

1. Open Banking → Bank Statement Import.
2. After operator approval, import the deterministic QA bank-statement CSV fixture.
3. Confirm the import reports acceptance and that every statement line appears with the same date, description, reference, debit and credit as the fixture.
4. Export the imported statement.
5. Compare the export against the fixture: MIME type, header row, column count, row count, the reference key column, and the debit/credit totals.
6. Re-submit the same fixture renamed with an image extension and confirm the import is rejected with a visible message.

Report as errors:
- Any statement line missing, duplicated, or altered after import.
- Debit or credit totals that differ from the fixture beyond the declared tolerance.
- An export with the wrong MIME type, missing headers, or a different row count.
- An invalid-MIME upload that is accepted.
- Any 4xx/5xx during upload or download, and any upload that appears to succeed but stores nothing.

On observer-only production tiers do not upload: assert the upload control is blocked and record that verdict.
