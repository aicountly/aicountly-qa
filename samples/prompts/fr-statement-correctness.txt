Financial Reporting correctness run.

1. Import the deterministic trial balance fixture.
2. Map every account to its Schedule III / IND-AS grouping.
3. Generate the balance sheet, statement of profit and loss, cash flow statement and the supporting notes.

Verify:
- The imported trial balance total debit equals total credit and equals the fixture totals.
- Every account in the fixture is mapped; report any unmapped or silently dropped account.
- Balance sheet total assets equal total equity and liabilities.
- Profit for the period on the P&L equals the figure carried into the balance sheet and the cash flow opening.
- Each note total equals the face-of-statement line it supports.
- Prior-period comparatives are carried through unchanged.
- The exported statement figures equal the on-screen figures.

Report every mismatch with the account or line item, the expected figure and the printed figure. Rounding differences beyond the declared tolerance are defects, not noise.
