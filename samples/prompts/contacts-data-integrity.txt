Contacts data-correctness run.

1. Create contacts and organisations with known names, emails, phone numbers, tags and addresses, including one record with accented characters and one with a very long name.
2. Re-open each record and compare every field against what was entered.
3. Import the deterministic contacts CSV fixture, then export contacts and compare row count, the email key column, and the header structure.
4. Search and filter for the created records by name, email and tag.
5. Merge or link two records if the product supports it, then confirm no field is lost.

Report as errors:
- Fields that come back trimmed, re-encoded, or empty after save.
- Duplicate records created by a single save.
- Imported rows that do not appear, or appear with shifted columns.
- Search that fails to return a record that demonstrably exists.
- Any console error, failed API call, or unsaved form.
