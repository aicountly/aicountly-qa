Secretarial compliance-data correctness run.

1. Create directors, shareholders and a share allotment with known dates, counts and nominal values.
2. Record a board meeting and a resolution against known dates.
3. Open the statutory registers (members, directors, charges, share transfers) and any MCA-form preview.
4. Export the member register.

Verify:
- Total issued shares equal the sum of the allotments recorded.
- Shareholding percentages sum to 100 and each holder's percentage matches their holding.
- Register entries appear in the correct chronological order with the entered dates.
- Meeting and resolution dates on the register match what was entered.
- The exported register has the same row count and the same key values as the on-screen register.

Report every mismatch with the register, the row, the expected value and the printed value, plus all console errors and failed API calls.
