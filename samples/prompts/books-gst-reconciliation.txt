GST reconciliation on Smart Books. Focus only on tax correctness.

1. Post sales and purchase vouchers at known taxable values across at least two GST rates, including one interstate transaction.
2. Open GSTR-1, GSTR-3B and the HSN summary for the same period.

Verify:
- Output CGST + SGST equals taxable sales × applicable rate for intrastate lines.
- Output IGST equals taxable sales × applicable rate for interstate lines.
- Input CGST + SGST + IGST equals taxable purchases × applicable rate.
- GSTR-1 outward taxable value equals the sum of sales voucher taxable amounts for the period.
- GSTR-3B liability equals GSTR-1 output tax for the same period.
- HSN summary quantity and taxable value reconcile with the item lines posted.
- Rounding follows one consistent rule; report any figure that differs by more than the declared tolerance.

Report every tax mismatch with the period, the voucher references involved, the expected figure and the printed figure. Skip permission, keyboard and UX sessions.
