Calendar scheduling correctness run.

1. Create single events, all-day events and a recurring series with known start/end times and a known timezone.
2. Invite an attendee, then reschedule and cancel one occurrence of the series.
3. Import the deterministic ICS fixture, then export the calendar.

Verify:
- Stored start and end times match what was entered, in the same timezone, after a page reload.
- A recurring series expands to the expected number of occurrences in the expected slots.
- Editing one occurrence does not silently modify the whole series (or vice versa, if that is the configured behaviour).
- The exported ICS contains the same UID, DTSTART, DTEND and RRULE values as the fixture.
- Conflicts and double bookings are detected where the product claims to detect them.

Report as errors any time shift, missing occurrence, dropped attendee, or ICS field lost on round trip, with expected versus actual.
